<idea>
**Design Movement:** Corporate Modernism meets Financial Empowerment
**Core Principles:**
- Trust through clarity — every element earns its place
- Asymmetric tension — hero section splits text left, visual right
- Elevation through contrast — deep navy anchors, crisp white lifts

**Color Philosophy:** Royal blue (#1E40AF) as the primary authority color, paired with sky blue (#3B82F6) for energy and white (#FFFFFF) for clarity. Light gray (#F8FAFC) for section backgrounds to avoid stark white fatigue.

**Layout Paradigm:** Diagonal section dividers create forward momentum. Hero is full-bleed with image overlay. Stats bar breaks the page with a deep blue band. Cards use subtle left-border accents.

**Signature Elements:**
- Diagonal section cuts between hero and content
- Blue gradient pill badges ("Free Consultation", "Verified Results")
- Thin horizontal rule with blue dot accent before section titles

**Interaction Philosophy:** Buttons scale on hover with a shadow lift. Cards tilt slightly on hover. CTA section pulses gently.

**Animation:** Fade-up entrance animations for section content. Counter animations for stats. Smooth scroll behavior.

**Typography System:** Playfair Display (bold, serif) for headlines — conveys authority and trust. Inter (clean, sans-serif) for body copy. Large display numbers in blue for stats.
</idea>

- Power through scale — large type, large imagery, large impact
- Blue as a statement of trust and ambition
- Layered depth — hero has image, overlay, and text all at different z-levels

**Color Philosophy:** Deep navy (#0F172A) for hero, royal blue (#1D4ED8) for CTAs, sky blue (#60A5FA) for highlights, pure white for text on dark, dark slate for text on light.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed hero with parallax background image. Sticky navigation with transparent-to-solid scroll behavior. Services in a 3-column card grid. Testimonials in a horizontal scroll carousel.

**Signature Elements:**
- Hero with full-bleed background image + blue gradient overlay
- Sticky nav that transitions from transparent to white on scroll
- Blue gradient CTA buttons with white text

**Interaction Philosophy:** Bold hover effects — buttons fill with gradient, cards get blue top border on hover. Navigation links get blue underline on hover.

**Animation:** Hero text slides in from left. Stats count up on scroll. Cards fade up staggered. Smooth scroll with section snap.

**Typography System:** Montserrat (bold, geometric) for headlines. Nunito Sans for body. All-caps tracking for section labels.
